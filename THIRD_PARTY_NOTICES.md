## Models and services used at runtime (not redistributed)

- **Meta Model API** (SAM 3.1, Muse Spark and other Meta models), called over HTTPS under Meta's terms for that API.
  No model weights are included in this repository.
- **SAM** is used through the Meta Model API, and its responses are decoded with `meta-sam-parser`, distributed by
  Meta under the SAM License. This project acknowledges the use of SAM Materials, as that licence asks of
  publications; the parser itself isn't redistributed here.
- The ArUco marker dictionary (`DICT_4X4_50`) comes from OpenCV.
